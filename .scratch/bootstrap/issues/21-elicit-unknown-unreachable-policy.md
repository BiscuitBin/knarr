# 21: Elicit: unknown and unreachable policy (§9.6)

**Context:** OVERVIEW §5 ("Unreachable, invalid or absent endpoints") and the §8 failure-mode table propose defaults for an unreachable pod, a timeout or 5xx, a 404 or never-implemented endpoint, an invalid payload, and a pod that has just started. §9.6 leaves open how long to keep the last value (in polls and in time) before the neutral removal 20 fixed, and how to detect an absent contract. It depends on the cost mapping (20).

**What to build:** Allium clauses for every poll outcome other than a valid payload, settled with the maintainer, including when knarr writes neutral and when it treats a pod as unmanaged.

**Non-goals:** Cleanup on uninstall or on leaving scope (23).

**Blocked by:** 20

**From 19:** [config.allium](../../../docs/specs/config.allium) leaves one open question for this ticket: whether the unreachable windows gain a per-Deployment override. If you add one, it is an annotation under `knarr.io/`, hyphenated after its `config.<key>`, parsed and invalidated under config's `Syntax`, `Effective` and `InvalidValue`, with a member added to `Settings` and `Defaults`; either way, close that question.

**From 30:** [observability.allium](../../../docs/specs/observability.allium) states the conventions any signal this ticket settles follows: metric names, labels and series life (`Catalogue` `Naming`, `Labels` and `SeriesLife`), Event reasons and type (`Events` `Conventions`), and log fields and levels (`Logs` `Format` and `Levels`). This ticket adds its own rows there with `tend` when it settles, without reopening 30 (`Catalogue` `LaterRows`). Already fixed: a pod entering the contract-absent state raises one create attempt of a `ContractAbsent` Event on its Deployment by name and uid, with the message `pod <pod>: no knarr status contract at port <port> path <path>; last reading <reading>` (`Events` `ContractAbsent`), counts on `knarr_pods_contract_absent{deployment}` (`Catalogue` `Standing`), and logs one warning line on entry (`Logs` `StateLines`). When a pod enters that state, and whether a restart enters it again, are this ticket's. Every poll outcome already counts on `knarr_polls_total{deployment, reading}` by Reading kind (`Polls`).

**MVP critical path:** yes. Unreachable and invalid pods are routine in production.

### Choices a maintainer may reverse

- The state names are `fresh` (last reading valid, or no reading yet in this process), `stale` (inside the window, value kept), `unknown` (window elapsed, neutral) and `absent`; "known" in prose means fresh or stale. The config names are `unknown_polls` and `absent_after`.
- `Readings` fixes the precedence when the clauses would disagree: a valid reading wins; otherwise a run that has lasted `absent_after` makes the pod absent; otherwise `failed` decides stale or unknown. `failed` keeps counting while absent and decides nothing there. `neutral` is true for exactly unknown and absent.
- The window values and the clock are parameters of every signature (`Pure`, as cost_mapping and budget do); the per-pod state is kept by the reconcile process 31 builds. `initial` takes no argument; the pinned checker (Allium 3.6.1) accepted `() -> PodState`.
- The absent run needs two readings at least, its first and the latest; after entry a reading that is not valid leaves `kind` absent and `run_since` as it is; a valid reading after absent returns the pod to fresh, and the next not_found or refused starts a new run.
- The unknown count runs for a pod with nothing applied too, so the gauge counts it; its neutral write is empty and sends nothing (ownership `WriteMode`).
- A terminating pod keeps its state machine while polled, since `observe` is pure over readings; its neutral write is dropped (k8s_client `NoPatchWhileTerminating`), the caller leaves it out of the entries it hands `entered` (observability `Cadence`) and the pod-state gauges exclude it (`SeriesLife`).
- The readiness clause is named `NotReadyWithCost`, not `Readiness`, to keep clear of `Probes` `Readiness` and `Diagnostics` `Readiness`; the gauge clause is `Gauges`.
- `knarr_pods_unknown` and `knarr_pods_contract_absent` are disjoint.
- `NotReadyWithCost` reads the applied value the marker records, so a NotReady pod found with a value above 0 after a restart is in the state from the first cycle until its window elapses; waiting for the first valid reading in the new process is one sentence to change.
- For safe-to-evict the neutral removal is the one rule in `Neutral`; 25 may replace that one sentence if it settles another value for a protection that ends for lack of data.
- `listed` means the same in config `Discovery` and budget `Leaving`: the whole target set, no-IP pods included; only the joining entries and the polled set exclude them.
- 31 is amended, not a new ticket, and no decision record is written. Dated "Amended by ticket 21" lines went to 18, 19, 20 and 24, as 20 added to 18, because 21 edited clauses those tickets own and not only the questions left for it; 30 needs none since `LaterRows` grants the row.
