# 46: Spec-then-build: the poll schedule and the patcher

**Context:** Ticket [24](24-elicit-poll-budget-feature-gate.md) settled the `budget` module in `docs/specs/budget.allium`: the `PollSchedule` contract (phase, delay-only jitter, bounded concurrency, no overlap, the LIST cadence, joining and leaving) and the `WriteBudget` contract (the per-pod patch interval, the global token bucket, the pending set, the exponential 429 pause and its install-wide scope), with ten install-wide values in its `config` block. Nothing runs a timer or holds a bucket yet: the poller ([43](43-status-poller.md)) sends one poll, the reconciler ([31](31-spec-then-build-reconcile-core.md)) decides whether a change is due, and neither owns when a poll fires or whether a due patch may go now.

**What to build:** The scheduler and the patcher from the `PollSchedule` and `WriteBudget` contracts in `docs/specs/budget.allium`. The scheduler: a per-pod schedule with a random phase and delay-only jitter, the concurrency bound with an oldest-due queue, no overlapping polls of one pod, and join and leave driven by the LIST and the LIST timer. The patcher: the token bucket, the per-pod interval counted from the last attempt whatever its outcome, the pending set served oldest-due first and re-derived from the latest reading when a pod's turn comes, the exponential 429 pause with Retry-After, and the LIST 429 case. The install-wide values come from OTP application configuration, as knarr's `Listening` reads the bind address, each with its default and valid range. Tests derived with `propagate` first, then the code in the order the `gleam-change` skill sets: pure functions for every contract signature with the clock and the random draw passed in, the scheduler and the patcher as processes under a supervisor, and FFI only for the clock if one is needed, in a named `*_ffi.erl` module.

**Non-goals:** What a poll reads and the timeout's enforcement (43). Whether a change is due and its re-derivation from the latest reading (31). The patch itself, the marker and other writers (32, 41). The harness that measures the write budget (33). The metric and Event names (30).

**Blocked by:** 24, 31, 41, 43

**From 24:** the hand-back notes of [ticket 24](24-elicit-poll-budget-feature-gate.md#hand-back-notes), its Settled table, and the `PollSchedule` and `WriteBudget` contracts.

**From 30:** [observability.allium](../../../docs/specs/observability.allium) names budget's six signals (`Catalogue` `Budget`, `Polls`), and [ticket 30's Settled table](30-elicit-observability.md#settled) rows 8 to 10 and 28 are the record. A 429 is counted once, as `result="throttled"` on `knarr_patches_total` or `knarr_lists_total`, never on a counter of its own; the results come from the status the client ([41](41-k8s-client-spec-then-build.md)) surfaces through `Catalogue` `patch_result` and `list_result`. `knarr_paused{scope}` and `knarr_pause_seconds{scope}` cover both pauses, scope `patch` for `BackOff` and `list` for `PauseScope`'s LIST pause. The per-Deployment series are retired when the Deployment leaves the target set (`SeriesLife`), and every count starts at 0 after a restart.

**MVP critical path:** yes. The write budget is a §3 success criterion, and 33 measures it against the bucket this ticket builds.

**Status:** ready-for-agent, round 2

- [ ] `Interval`, `Phase` and `Jitter` have tests by value: `phase` puts a joining pod's first poll at now plus 0 to interval less one second, for a 30-second interval and for a 5-second one; `next` puts the next poll at the previous due time plus the interval plus 0 to 10% of it rounded down to whole seconds, so 30 to 33 seconds after a 30-second due time and never earlier; a rung already past is skipped and the next poll is due on the first rung after now, each rung the previous plus the interval plus a fresh draw, so a poll due at 0 that returns at 40 with a 30-second interval and no jitter is next due at 60, not 40; the interval is the Deployment's override or else `poll_interval`.
- [ ] `NoOverlap` and `Concurrency`: a poll due while the pod's previous poll is outstanding is skipped, and the following poll is due on the next rung of the ladder counted from the skipped due time; `admit` is true at 63 sent and false at 64 with the default bound; a due poll not admitted stays outstanding in a queue served oldest-due first, and nothing leaves the queue except as `Leaving` says.
- [ ] `ListCadence`, `Leaving` and `Restart`: the pod, ReplicaSet and Deployment LISTs (config `Discovery`, ticket 19) form one discovery cycle on a timer that fires every `list_interval`, 60 seconds by default, whatever the poll intervals, and a cycle in which any LIST of the cycle fails is retried whole on the next tick that is sent, so not on a tick falling inside the LIST pause; `on_list` drops the schedule of a pod the LIST no longer shows, a queued poll of that pod leaves the queue at once and is never sent, the response to one already in flight is discarded when it returns, `on_list` keeps a pod with `deletionTimestamp` set on the schedule, and adds, for every listed pod that has none, the schedule the caller built with `phase` from that pod's Deployment interval and its own draw, ignoring one built for a pod already scheduled; after a restart every phase is drawn afresh and the marker timestamp is not read.
- [ ] `Bucket` and `PerPod`: `refill` adds whole seconds only, 5 tokens per elapsed second by default, and advances `refilled_at` by exactly the seconds it credited, never to now: from `refilled_at` 0 a call at 0.5 adds nothing and leaves `refilled_at` at 0, and a call at 1.5 adds 5 tokens and sets `refilled_at` to 1, so the half second is carried; the bucket is capped at the burst of 10; `allow` needs one token; `allow` is false inside the patch interval, 60 seconds by default, of the pod's last attempt whatever its outcome, and is not held by the interval when `last_attempt` is null.
- [ ] `BackOff` and `PauseScope`: the first 429 pauses 1 second, consecutive 429s pause 2, 4, 8, 16, 32 and then 60 seconds at the cap; a Retry-After of 120 seconds beats the cap; a pause is never shortened, so a bare 429 one second after a Retry-After of 120 leaves `until` at 120; a 2xx resets `consecutive` to 0 and leaves `until` as it is, and a 403 between two 429s leaves the run as it is; while paused `allow` is false for every pod of the install; the discovery cycle keeps a pause of its own under the same rule: a 429 on any LIST of the cycle skips the whole cycle, keeps the previous target set and schedules, and lengthens the LIST pause, a cycle whose tick falls inside that pause is not sent, the run ends when a cycle completes with every LIST of it succeeding, and a patch pause never holds the cycle nor the reverse; polls are never paused.
- [ ] `Pending`: at most one pending entry per pod, kept from when the change first became due and re-derived from the latest reading when the pod's turn comes; `order` serves oldest-due first with no priority for a drained or terminating change and no rotation by Deployment; a pending entry is discarded when a LIST first shows the pod's `deletionTimestamp` set or no longer shows the pod.
- [ ] `Threshold`: a test drives the bucket at the worst case and shows at most 5·W + 10 patches in any W-second window at the defaults within one run of knarr, so 310 in a minute; a restart refills the bucket, so a window spanning one may see up to 10 more.
- [ ] The ten install-wide values are read from OTP application configuration with their defaults and valid ranges: poll interval 5 to 300 s (30), timeout 1 s to below the poll interval (5 s), jitter 0 to 100 percent (10), LIST 10 to 600 s (60 s), concurrency 1 to 1024 (64), patch interval 0 to 3600 s (60 s; a per-Deployment override has the same range), rate 1 to 100 per second (5), burst 1 to 200 (10), back-off initial 1 s to the back-off cap (1 s) and cap 1 to 600 s (60 s). What happens on a value outside its range is a clause written with `tend` before it is coded; budget states only the ranges.
- [ ] The failed-patch and failed-list values [41](41-k8s-client-spec-then-build.md) returns, carrying the status and the Retry-After header in seconds, feed `pause`; 41 is in this ticket's Blocked by for that reason. `NoGateCheck` holds: nothing in the scheduler or the patcher requests the feature gate's state or reads `/metrics`, and the Role gains no verb for it. The clock and the random draw are injected and every signature of both contracts is pure (`Pure`); the scheduler and the patcher run under a supervisor; any FFI is a named `*_ffi.erl` module.
- [ ] The metrics [30](30-elicit-observability.md) names for the budget are emitted (observability `Catalogue` `Budget` and `Polls`): the patch rate as `knarr_patches_total{deployment, purpose, result}`; the pending changes as `knarr_patches_pending{deployment}` and `knarr_patch_pending_oldest_seconds`, the age of the oldest `due_since`, 0 when nothing is pending; a 429 as `result="throttled"` on `knarr_patches_total` and on `knarr_lists_total{resource, result}`, never on a separate counter; the pause state as `knarr_paused{scope}`, 0 or 1, and `knarr_pause_seconds{scope}`, the length of the current pause as last set and 0 when not paused, with scope `patch` or `list`; the poll queue depth as `knarr_poll_queue_depth`; and the skipped polls as `knarr_polls_skipped_total{deployment}`. This ticket is the one emitter of `knarr_patches_total`, `knarr_lists_total` and `knarr_poll_duration_seconds`, since it sends every patch, LIST and poll; [32](32-spec-then-build-ownership-lifecycle.md) asserts the patch results its invariants raise and [31](31-spec-then-build-reconcile-core.md) counts the readings. `knarr_poll_duration_seconds` uses the fixed `Buckets` bounds 0.01 to 60 seconds and +Inf, not derived from config, and a `timed_out` poll is observed at the timeout.
- [ ] `just check` passes and the hand-back gives the `plan-spec` obligation count.
