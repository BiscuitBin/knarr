# 47: Discovery's cleanup hand-off

**Context:** Ticket [23](23-elicit-cleanup-staleness-restart.md) settled `docs/specs/lifecycle.allium` after [31](31-spec-then-build-reconcile-core.md) had built the reconcile core, and its `From 23` paragraph in 31 gives 31's discovery process the work of building lifecycle's `View` for every listed pod and handing it to `Cleanup` `due`. That work calls the `Cleanup` contract and ownership's `cleanup`, which [32](32-spec-then-build-ownership-lifecycle.md) builds, and sends through the pending set [46](46-spec-then-build-poll-schedule-and-patcher.md) builds, so 31 merged without it; [31's hand-back](31-spec-then-build-reconcile-core.md#after-merging-main-ticket-23) lists what is missing. Until this lands nothing runs cleanup: the pods of a Deployment that opted out or was deleted keep knarr's annotations and marker, and a safe-to-evict whose opt-in was removed stays.

**What to build:** The wiring between the three LISTs and lifecycle's contracts, in the reconciler as 46 leaves it, test first in the order the `gleam-change` skill sets. No clause changes: lifecycle, ownership, budget and observability already state every behaviour below, and a gap found is a `spec-change`, not a judgement call in code.

**Non-goals:** `Cleanup` `due` and `done` and ownership's `cleanup`, which 32 builds. The pending set, the bucket, the per-pod interval and the pause, which 46 builds. The rejection signals and their cadence (`CleanupRejected`, `knarr_patch_rejections_total`), which 32 builds; this ticket feeds them a cleanup's outcome. Lifecycle's `Restart` and `Uninstall` beyond the first cycle below (32, 34). The pod-uid open question in `ownership.allium` (32).

**Blocked by:** 32, 46

**From 31:** [31's hand-back](31-spec-then-build-reconcile-core.md#after-merging-main-ticket-23), section "After merging main (ticket 23)". `src/knarr/reconciler.gleam` runs the discovery cycle, keeps the per-pod memory, rebuilds the gauges each cycle and retires a Deployment's series once no current target carries its name (`state.targets`); `knarr_annotations` counts target pods only. config's `leaving` is retired, with its log line.

**From 23:** [lifecycle.allium](../../../docs/specs/lifecycle.allium) `Cleanup`: the `View`, `Held` and `Removal` value types and the `Scope`, `Marker`, `Retry`, `Order`, `Done`, `Label` and `Pure` invariants; the `From 23` paragraphs of 31, 32 and 46, and [23's Settled table](23-elicit-cleanup-staleness-restart.md#settled).

**MVP critical path:** yes. Without it the MVP never cleans up after an opt-out, a deleted Deployment or a removed safe-to-evict opt-in, which lifecycle promises (§9.12); 33's restart threshold, whose opt-out made during the downtime must be cleaned up, and 40 wait for it.

**Status:** ready-for-agent, round 2

- [ ] Each discovery cycle in which every LIST succeeded builds one `View` per pod the unselected pod LIST shows: `target` from config's target set; `enabled` pod-deletion-cost always and safe-to-evict when `Settings.safe_to_evict` is true, empty for a pod that is not a target; `terminating` from `deletionTimestamp`; `deployment` the name the pod's ReplicaSet's controller owner reference carries (k8s_client `Owner.name`), empty when the chain names no Deployment; and `held` from ownership `read` and `standing` on the newest API-returned view of the pod (lifecycle `View`, ownership `Adoption`). A failed cycle builds none and changes nothing (lifecycle `Scope`). Tests: a target, a pod of an opted-out Deployment, a pod of a deleted Deployment, a cascade-orphaned pod with `deployment` empty, a terminating pod, a pod with no marker, and `held` following knarr's own accepted patch.
- [ ] Every `View` is handed to `Cleanup` `due`, and a pod with any Removal has one pending change with purpose `cleanup` under budget's `order`, at most one per pod and purpose, kept from when it first became due (budget `Pending`, lifecycle `Order`); `Pending` stores no annotation name and no deployment, so the View's `deployment` is kept beside the entry while it is pending or in flight, for `Done` and `Label`. When its turn comes the cleanup is re-derived from the newest view and every name `due` then returns goes to ownership's `cleanup` together, one patch for the pod (lifecycle `Marker`, ownership `Released`), and it counts on `knarr_patches_total` with purpose `cleanup` under that deployment label (lifecycle `Label`). Tests: an opt-out of a pod owning both annotations makes one pending entry and one patch that removes both and the marker; an opt-out removes the cost and the marker; a removed safe-to-evict opt-in removes that annotation alone and keeps the marker; a yielded record keeps the marker; the null record reconcile's `Neutral` leaves gets the marker-only patch; a pod due both an apply and a cleanup makes two patches, the second a patch interval after the first.
- [ ] A cleanup that is not accepted leaves only the pod's last attempt time; it is due again from every later view that names it, no sooner than the patch interval, `config.patch_interval` for a pod that is not a target, and never inside a pause; a rejection enters 32's `CleanupRejected` run and counts on `knarr_patch_rejections_total` with purpose `cleanup` (lifecycle `Retry`). Tests: a 422 rebuilt and accepted after the next LIST; a 403 twice raising one Event; an accepted patch ending the run.
- [ ] A Deployment's series retire only once it is not in the target set, nothing for its pods is pending with purpose apply or in flight with purpose apply or yield, and `Cleanup` `done` is true for its name, with outstanding built from the pending set and the patches in flight (observability `SeriesLife`, lifecycle `Done`). This replaces 31's retirement on leaving the target set alone. Tests: an opt-out's series last until its cleanup is accepted; a cleanup in flight for a pod that has left the pod LIST holds them; the series of the empty name live and retire as a Deployment's do.
- [ ] `knarr_annotations` counts a pod that is not a target by its View's `deployment`, annotations standing owned only, so a value an opt-out leaves counts until cleanup drops its record and a yielded or conflict record counts nowhere (observability `Standing`).
- [ ] After a restart the first discovery cycle in which every LIST succeeded derives every cleanup due at once, with no startup pass of its own (lifecycle `Restart` `NoStartupStep`, budget `Restart`).
